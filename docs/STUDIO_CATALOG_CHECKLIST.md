# Shower Studio - catalog & price checklist

Generated from the starter catalog (research of 2026-10-11). Every starter product is imported as an **unpublished draft with no price**. Nothing is shown to customers until you publish it, and no price is shown until you enter one.

How to use: Admin -> **Studio Catalog** -> Import starter products (once). Then for each product you sell: open it, add a photo (and for walls a texture), enter the price, check the specs, **Publish**. Or fill in `STUDIO_PRICE_SHEET.csv` and enter the numbers from it.

## 4. Before publishing anything

- Use only photos and textures you are licensed to use (your own photos, or manufacturer images with permission).
- Prices you enter are shown to customers as an **estimated total** - so publish only products you actually sell at that price.
- A published product without a price makes designs that use it show **"Price confirmed after review."** instead of a number.
- Packages: optional. A package price replaces the individual prices of its products when a design contains all of them.
